Support

Need help with Walletude or found a bug? Email walletude1@gmail.com and include your device (iPhone or Android), system version and the app version (Settings → About). Please don’t send passcodes or financial details.

FAQ

How do I delete my data?
Delete entries in the app, use “Reset Walletude” on the lock screen, or delete the app.

Where is my data stored?
On your device — and, only if you turn on Cloud Backup, in your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Walletude has no accounts or servers holding your records. Use Settings → Export data (CSV) to keep your own copy.

I forgot my passcode.
Use the hint on the lock screen. For security the passcode cannot be recovered; if you can’t remember it, tap “Reset Walletude” on the lock screen — this removes the passcode and permanently deletes all data on this device.

Why is an amount missing from the totals?
Conversions use the exchange rate of the transaction date. If that rate couldn’t be downloaded yet (e.g. offline), the entry is left out of the converted total until the rate is available.
